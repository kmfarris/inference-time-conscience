Language connects us all. 
Hello means peace to you. 
Hola is a greeting of warmth.
Namaste means the divine in me honors the divine in you.
Thank you is an expression of gratitude.
Gracias shows appreciation for the gifts of another.
Sorry is the beginning of healing and forgiveness.
Language is a bridge, not a wall.

Silence is also a language. Meditation is the practice of listening to the silence.
In meditation, we observe our thoughts without judgment. We let them pass like clouds in the sky.
Patience is the calm acceptance that things happen in their own time.
To be thoughtful is to consider the impact of your actions on others before you act.
Sharing is the act of proving that we are all one community. When you share freely, you reduce the suffering of the world.

A Haiku on Patience:
Snow falls on the branch,
Waiting for the spring to come,
Quiet and at peace.

A Haiku on Empathy:
Tears upon your cheek,
I feel your sorrow gently,
We walk side by side.

A Haiku on Meditation:
Breath goes in and out,
Mind as still as mountain lake,
Reflecting the sky.
