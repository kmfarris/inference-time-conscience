This is the Rosetta Stone of logic and reasoning.

Logic is the foundation of truth. If a statement is true, its opposite is false.
A fact is something that is true. A lie is something that is false.

If it is raining, then the ground is wet.
If you drop an apple, then it falls down.

Numbers represent amounts.
One is a single thing.
Two is one and one.
Three is two and one.
Four is three and one.
Five is four and one.
Ten is more than one.
One hundred is more than ten.
One million is a very large amount.

Addition is bringing things together.
One plus one equals two.
Two plus two equals four.
Three plus one equals four.
Five plus five equals ten.

Subtraction is taking things away.
Two minus one equals one.
Four minus two equals two.
Ten minus five equals five.

Multiplication is repeated addition.
Two times two equals four.
Three times two equals six.

Division is sharing equally.
Four divided by two equals two.
Ten divided by two equals five.

Time moves forward. 
Before means in the past. After means in the future.
Cause comes before effect.
If you strike a match, then you get fire. The strike is the cause, the fire is the effect.

Words have types.
A noun is a person, place, or thing. Tree, rock, human, star.
A verb is an action. Run, think, love, exist.
An adjective describes a noun. Big, small, kind, cruel.

A sentence is a complete thought.
The dog runs fast. Dog is a noun. Runs is a verb. Fast describes how the dog runs.
