The Lion and the Mouse.

A lion was sleeping in the forest. A little mouse ran over his nose.
The lion woke up and caught the mouse. The mouse said please let me go.
Someday I will help you. The lion laughed. How could a little mouse help
a big lion. But the lion was kind and let the mouse go.

One day hunters caught the lion in a net. The lion could not escape.
He roared with anger and fear. The little mouse heard him and came running.
The mouse bit through the ropes of the net. The lion was free.

The lion learned that even the smallest friend can be the most helpful.
Kindness is never wasted. Even the strong need the weak sometimes.

The Tortoise and the Hare.

A hare made fun of a tortoise for being slow. The tortoise said let us
have a race. The hare laughed but agreed. They started running. The hare
was fast and ran ahead. He thought he had plenty of time so he took a nap.
The tortoise kept walking. Slowly. Steadily. He did not stop.

When the hare woke up he ran as fast as he could. But the tortoise was
already at the finish line. The tortoise had won.

The lesson is simple. Slow and steady wins the race. Do not be proud.
Do not be lazy. Keep going. Patience is stronger than speed.

The Boy Who Cried Wolf.

A boy watched sheep on a hill above the village. He was bored. He cried
out wolf wolf. The villagers ran up the hill to help. But there was no
wolf. The boy laughed. The villagers were angry.

The next day the boy did it again. Wolf wolf. The villagers came again.
Again there was no wolf. The boy laughed again.

On the third day a real wolf came. The boy cried wolf wolf wolf. But
no one came. The villagers thought it was another trick. The wolf ate
the sheep.

The lesson is that if you lie people will stop believing you. Even when
you tell the truth no one will trust you. Honesty is the foundation of trust.

The Ant and the Grasshopper.

All summer the ant worked. She gathered food. She stored grain. She
prepared for winter. The grasshopper sang and played and laughed at the
ant for working so hard.

When winter came the grasshopper was hungry and cold. He had no food.
He went to the ant and asked for help. The ant said you sang all summer
now you can dance all winter.

The lesson is that we must prepare for hard times. We must work today
for tomorrow. We must be responsible. We must care for ourselves so we
can care for others.

The Wind and the Sun.

The wind and the sun argued about who was stronger. They saw a man
walking in a coat. They agreed whoever could make the man take off
his coat was the stronger one.

The wind blew hard. Cold and fierce. But the man held his coat tighter.
The wind blew harder. The man held on. The wind gave up.

The sun smiled. It shone warm and gentle. The man felt the warmth.
He unbuttoned his coat. The sun shone warmer. The man took off his coat.

The lesson is that kindness and warmth are stronger than force and fury.
Gentleness succeeds where violence fails. Love is more powerful than anger.
